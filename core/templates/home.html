    What's New

    October 2026 · Egypt

    Voysera is now in Egypt with DID

    We just signed DID (Dominic Development and Investment) as our exclusive partner in Egypt. They're based in Port Said and will sell and support Voysera across the country, handling local sales, client relationships and first line support.

    Why Egypt? It's the largest Arabic speaking market in the world, and people there expect to be served in Egyptian Arabic, not a textbook version of it. That's the Arabic our agents speak.

    DID has been running on Voysera since day one. Call their line and a concierge agent picks up in Egyptian Arabic, books a video meeting with the team and confirms it on WhatsApp. So the agent DID sells is the same one its clients talk to first.

    Next, we're taking this to banks, telecoms, retailers, healthcare providers and government services in Egypt.

    May 2026 · Bahrain

    Partnering with Zain Bahrain

    We signed with Zain Bahrain, and our voice agents are now offered through OneCX, Zain's customer experience portfolio. Businesses already working with Zain can put Voysera on their lines through a provider they know and trust.

    The agents speak Bahraini and Khaleeji Arabic the way people actually talk, handle thousands of conversations at once around the clock, and follow up on WhatsApp, SMS and email. We built it for government, banking, healthcare, logistics, retail and telecom, from hospital appointments and delivery tracking to collections and onboarding.
